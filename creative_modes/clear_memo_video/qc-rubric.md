# Director QC Rubric

Technical QC is necessary but insufficient. Before a work is marked ready, review it against these questions:

1. **Recognition**: does the first three seconds contain a concrete, understandable tension?
2. **Observation**: is there one genuine observation, with fact and interpretation kept distinct?
3. **Narrative movement**: does each beat advance the thought, rather than repeat the same sentence over another image?
4. **Visual honesty**: are real assets identified honestly, and are illustrations clearly expressive rather than fake evidence?
5. **Rhythm**: do voice duration, cuts, subtitles and pauses feel intentionally aligned?
6. **Identity**: would the work still feel like this account without its logo or title?
7. **Anti-template check**: does it avoid PPT, generic AI image carousel, lecture tone and motivational-content shortcuts?

If a work fails Recognition, Observation, Visual honesty, or Identity, it is `NOT_READY` even when the MP4 is technically valid. Record concise reasons and rework only the failing layer.
