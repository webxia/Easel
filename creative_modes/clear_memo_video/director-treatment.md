# Director Treatment

`clear_memo_video` is a film-language mode, not a writing formula. Keep the topic, argument and story structure open.

Before writing shots, make two compact decisions:

1. **Content Core**: classify each important statement as real experience, current action, learning, public fact, or unverified thought. State the one tension the viewer should recognise.
2. **Director Treatment**: decide the viewer's emotional path, the first concrete image or line, the moment of recognition, and where the work should stop without forcing a solution.

The preferred posture is an ordinary person noticing something specific, not an expert delivering a lesson. Open from a lived detail, a calculation, a small contradiction, or a changed understanding. Do not manufacture hardship, dialogue, professional incidents, family scenes, or outcomes to make the narrative complete.

Do not force a hook type, three-act structure, CTA, or conclusion. A quiet ending, an unresolved question, or one next action is valid when it fits the material.
