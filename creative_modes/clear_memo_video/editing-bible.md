# Editing Bible

The default rhythm is unhurried but never static: each visual beat earns its screen time, and each cut changes the viewer's understanding, setting or emotional distance.

- Prefer 25-60 seconds and roughly 6-9 visual beats as a starting range, not a fixed template.
- Establish the concrete tension in the first three seconds. Do not spend the opening on a channel ident, abstract slogan or generic setup.
- Avoid rapid montage, decorative transitions, bouncing captions and repeated near-identical images.
- Keep subtitles concise, readable and inside the 9:16 safe area. The subtitle pace follows speech, not an arbitrary visual beat grid.
- Let the last image and last sentence have room to land. Do not force a call to action, a solution or an optimistic moral.
